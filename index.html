About SalamX

An ethical technology company inspired by Islamic values, delivering innovative digital solutions with professionalism, transparency, quality, and trust.

Technology Guided by Values

SalamX Limited is a premium technology consulting and software development company based in Sokoto, Nigeria. We are driven by a commitment to ethical practices rooted in Islamic principles of honesty, excellence, and service to humanity.

Our team partners with businesses, startups, NGOs, educational institutions, and government organisations to design and deliver secure, scalable, and impactful digital solutions — from custom software and websites to AI systems, cloud platforms, and end-to-end digital transformation.

Every engagement is built on transparency, rigorous quality standards, and long-term partnership. We believe technology should empower people and organisations while upholding integrity and social responsibility.
